# CampusHelp – Risk Register and Quality Plan

## 1. Risk Register

The following risks were identified during the planning of the CampusHelp project.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Probability | Impact | Risk Owner | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | A team member may become unavailable or fail to complete assigned tasks on time. | Medium | High | Project Manager | Distribute responsibilities clearly, monitor progress, and redistribute tasks if necessary. |
| R02 | Delays in designing the student request interface and admin dashboard. | High | Medium | Business Analyst & Designer | Prioritize essential screens and follow the agreed project schedule. |
| R03 | GitHub access or collaboration problems may delay communication and file submissions. | Medium | Medium | Project Manager | Check repository access, send invitations, and communicate regularly with team members. |
| R04 | Changes in project requirements may increase the project scope, time, and workload. | Medium | High | Project Manager | Document every change request, evaluate its impact, and obtain team approval before implementation. |
| R05 | Errors or missing information may be identified during prototype testing. | Medium | High | Quality & Risk Coordinator | Review the prototype, prepare test cases, and document test results before final submission. |

## 2. Most Serious Risk

Risk R04 – Changes in project requirements – is considered one of the most serious risks because changes may affect the project scope, schedule, budget, and team workload.

For example, the request to add an automatic email acknowledgment after submission requires additional work. The team must evaluate the change, estimate the additional hours and cost, and decide whether it can be completed within the project timeline.

## 3. Quality Plan

The purpose of the quality plan is to ensure that the CampusHelp project documents and prototype meet the required project expectations.

The team will use the following quality checks:

1. **Documentation Review:** Check that project documents are complete, clear, and properly organized.
2. **Required Fields:** Verify that the student request form includes all necessary fields.
3. **Category Selection:** Check that students can select the appropriate request category.
4. **Priority Selection:** Verify that Low, Medium, and High priority levels are represented.
5. **Submission Confirmation:** Check that the proposed process includes a confirmation message after submission.
6. **Request Status Visibility:** Verify that students can view request statuses such as Open, In Progress, and Completed.
7. **Administrator Dashboard:** Check that administrators can view submitted requests and their statuses.
8. **Test Case Review:** Review all six test cases and document their results.

## 4. Quality Acceptance Criteria

The project will be considered ready for final submission when:

- All required project documents are prepared.
- The student request interface and administrator dashboard are represented in the prototype.
- At least six test cases are documented.
- Identified risks have owners and mitigation strategies.
- The final project report is reviewed by the team.
- Any simulated functionality is clearly identified.

## 5. Conclusion

The risk register identifies five potential risks that may affect the CampusHelp project. Each risk has an assigned owner and a mitigation strategy.

The quality plan provides a structured approach to reviewing project documentation, prototype screens, and test cases. Regular communication, progress monitoring, and quality checks will help the team complete the project successfully within the planned timeline.

**Note:** The prototype testing is simulated and does not represent a fully developed production application.
