3. Testing Review
Six prototype test cases were documented. All six were marked as Pass (simulated).
The tests cover required fields, category selection, priority selection, submission confirmation, request status visibility, and the administrator dashboard.
The testing results are simulated and do not represent a fully deployed application.
4. Risk Review
Five project risks were identified, including team member unavailability, design delays, GitHub collaboration problems, changing requirements, and prototype defects.
Mitigation strategies and responsible owners were documented in the Risk Register.
5. Change Request Review
The proposed automatic email acknowledgment was evaluated.
The team recommended deferring actual email integration to a future development phase.
Estimated additional effort: 3 hours.
Estimated additional cost: 150,000 UZS.
Decision: DEFER.
6. Outstanding Items
The team must verify that the wireframes, student request interface, and administrator dashboard are completed and included in the final submission.
Any unfinished deliverables should be documented before project closure.
7. Final Verification Conclusion
The main project management and quality documentation has been prepared and submitted through GitHub.
The project has documented its schedule, budget, scope, risks, testing activities, and change request decision.
Final closure should take place after all remaining deliverables have been reviewed and verified.
