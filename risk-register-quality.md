2. Most Serious Risk
R04 – Requirements may change during the project.
This is considered one of the most serious risks because additional requirements may affect the project scope, schedule, budget, and workload. The team will evaluate every proposed change before approving it.
3. Quality Plan
The Quality & Risk Coordinator will coordinate quality assurance activities.
The team will:
Review project documents for completeness and consistency.
Check that the student request form contains all required fields.
Verify category and priority selection in the prototype.
Check submission confirmation and request status visibility.
Review administrator dashboard information.
Record test results and document simulated functionality.
4. Quality Acceptance Criteria
All required project documents are available.
The prototype covers the main CampusHelp requirements.
At least six test cases are documented.
Identified risks have owners and mitigation strategies.
Final deliverables are reviewed before submission.
5. Conclusion
The risk register identifies five key project risks and provides mitigation strategies. The quality plan establishes the checks required to review the CampusHelp prototype and project documentation.
